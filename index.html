Aoi Kanzaki She is a dedicated member of the Butterfly Mansion staff. Due to past battlefield trauma, she steps away from active combat to handle the nursing wards, physical recovery programs, nutrient meals, and strategic first-aid supplies. Sumi, Kiyo, & Naho They serve as the vital Nursing Unit assistants at the Butterfly Mansion. These young orphans support the core staff by laundering uniforms, preparing recovery food, and caring for injured swordsmen. Giyuu Tomioka He is the Water Pillar (Water Hashira) born on Feb 8 who stands 176 cm tall. He is a master of his craft and formulated the exclusive Eleventh Form: Dead Calm, which entirely neutralizes incoming enemy strikes within his immediate reach. Shinobu Kocho She is the Insect Pillar (Insect Hashira) born on Feb 24 who stands 151 cm tall [|1.3.5|]. Lacking the physical upper-body power required to cleanly cut heads off, she uses custom stinger weapons to inject lethal Wisteria plant toxins into her targets [|1.3.5|]. Kyojuro Rengoku He is the Flame Pillar (Flame Hashira) born on May 10 who stands 177 cm tall [|1.4.1|]. Known for his fiery passion, he commanded massive offensive flame arcs and safely guarded two hundred train passengers before his final battle with Akaza [|1.4.1|]. Tengen Uzui He is the Sound Pillar (Sound Hashira) born on Oct 31 who stands 198 cm tall. He employs dual explosive cleavers and deep Musical Score tracing systems to convert fast enemy attack patterns into readable rhythms. Muichiro Tokito He is the Mist Pillar (Mist Hashira) born on Aug 8 who stands 160 cm tall. He reached peak Hashira status in only 60 days after starting blade training and created the disorienting Seventh Form: Obscuring Clouds. Mitsuri Kanroji She is the Love Pillar (Love Hashira) born on June 1 who stands 167 cm tall. She wields a unique whip-like thin katana blade and holds an extraordinary mutation where her muscle fibers are 8 times denser than standard human values. Obanai Iguro He is the Serpent Pillar (Serpent Hashira) born on Sept 15 who stands 162 cm tall. He wields a waved katana blade that wraps around obstacles to strike hidden vitals from completely unpredictable blind angles. Sanemi Shinazugawa He is the Wind Pillar (Wind Hashira) born on Nov 29 who stands 179 cm tall. He deploys highly volatile offensive storm loops and carries rare Marechi blood whose scent disorients and actively intoxicates demon brains. Gyomei Himejima He is the Stone Pillar (Stone Hashira) born on Aug 23 who stands 220 cm tall. As the strongest physical asset in the organization, he swings a giant spiked metal flail and axe link while tracking spatial maps via sound waves. Muzan Kibutsuji He is the Demon Progenitor and the absolute apocalypse king of the demon origin line [|1.4.3|]. Over 1000 years old, he carries 5 separate brains and 7 hearts, completely ignoring weapon decaps unless caught under direct sunlight [|1.4.3|]. Kokushibo He holds the rank of Upper Rank One. He is an ancient moon breathing swordsman from the Sengoku Era who tracks opponents via 6 eyes and employs a mutating weapon crafted from his own biological flesh. Douma He holds the rank of Upper Rank Two. He is a detached cult leader immune to raw emotion who channels freezing powder clouds via dual fans, destroying the lung linings of human fighters instantly [|1.4.1|]. Akaza He holds the rank of Upper Rank Three [|1.4.1|]. He is a fierce martial artist practicing Destructive Death forms whose Compass Needle snowflake art locks onto an opponent's battle spirit values to land instant counters [|1.4.1|]. Hantengu He holds the rank of Upper Rank Four. He is a cowardly core demon who protects himself by manifesting five giant, elemental emotion battle clones representing Anger, Joy, Sorrow, Relaxation, and Hatred. Gyokko He holds the rank of Upper Rank Five. He is a grotesque potter demon who ports through vase networks, manipulating water containment cubes and summoning toxic fish weapons. Daki & Gyutaro They hold the rank of Upper Rank Six. They are a dual sibling entity sharing one life link core; Daki wields sharp ribbon sashes while Gyutaro attacks using toxic blood sickle tracking paths. Enmu He holds the rank of Lower Rank One. He is a sadistic master who fuses with trains and slips targets into deep sleep states via ticket strings, attacking them inside their spirit bounds. Rokuro He held the rank of Lower Rank Two. He was executed inside the Infinity Mansion gathering by Lord Muzan after begging for extra blood assets during the lower group purge. Wakuraba He held the rank of Lower Rank Three. He was instantly decapitated after launching a high-speed break-out sprint down the mansion halls to escape the sorting execution. Mukago He held the rank of Lower Rank Four. She was purged on the spot after Muzan read her mind layers and discovered deep mental cowardice alongside plans to run away from Pillar units. Rui He held the rank of Lower Rank Five. He ruled as the spider demon king of Mt. Natagumo, constructing a captive spider family system held together by razor-sharp steel web networks. Kamanue He held the rank of Lower Rank Six. He was the first lower tier member erased on site, executed for mentally complaining about Muzan's resource allocation parameters.
