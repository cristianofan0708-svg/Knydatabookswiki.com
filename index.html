Tanjiro Kamado

Corps Member Entry // #001

History

Originally a peaceful charcoal seller in the mountains, Tanjiro joined the Demon Slayer Corps after his family was tragically slaughtered by Muzan Kibutsuji, leaving his sister Nezuko turned into a demon. He trained under Sakonji Urokodaki to master the blade.
